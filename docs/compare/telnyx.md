# Moving from Telnyx to SMS Mobile App

Telnyx bills a per-part rate plus the carrier's fee, for software built on its API. SMS Mobile App is for people who are not building software.

## What stays behind

Your API integration and inbound messages stay behind, and foreign destinations stay on Telnyx. Recipients will see your SIM's number instead.
