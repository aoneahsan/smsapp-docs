# Moving from SmsGateWay24 to SMS Mobile App

SmsGateWay24 is a workspace with SMS and WhatsApp chats, orders and invoices. SMS Mobile App sends reviewed SMS and nothing else around it.

## What stays behind

Chats, WhatsApp, team access, orders and invoices stay behind, as does any use of its API. Keep your own copy of whatever you need from them before you cancel.
