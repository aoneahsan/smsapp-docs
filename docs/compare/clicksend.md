ClickSend offers mass texting, email to SMS and an API through its own service. SMS Mobile App sends from your own SIM after you read a review screen.

## What stays behind

Email to SMS, inbound replies and API sends stay behind. Its rates sit behind a calculator we could not read, so get your own figure from ClickSend before you compare.
