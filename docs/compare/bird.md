# Moving from Bird (formerly MessageBird) to SMS Mobile App

Bird runs SMS, email and WhatsApp on a funded balance. SMS Mobile App is SMS from your own SIM, to Pakistani numbers, on a plan.

## What stays behind

Your balance, rented numbers, inbox and other channels stay behind, and so does API access. Use up or withdraw the balance under Bird's own terms before you stop.
