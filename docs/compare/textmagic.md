# Moving from Textmagic to SMS Mobile App

Textmagic gives you a rented texting number and per-text credit, mostly for the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. SMS Mobile App sends to Pakistani numbers from your own SIM.

## What stays behind

The rented number, its inbound replies and any remaining credit stay behind. Contacts outside Pakistan cannot move at all.
