# Sign in with Google

1. Open SMSApp on Android and choose Google sign-in.
2. Select the project-authorized owner account.
3. Open the web admin and sign in with the same account.
4. Confirm that the account identity matches on both surfaces before registering or selecting a phone.

Signing in authenticates the owner. It does not grant SMS permission, upload contacts, enable the sender, or start a job. If the two surfaces use different accounts, stop and sign out before creating data under the wrong owner.
