# Google Play SMS permission status

The Android app `com.aoneahsan.smsapp` ("SMS Mobile App") is being prepared for its Google Play release ([Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aoneahsan.smsapp)).

The Android app uses `SEND_SMS` for its core device-automation function, operated by the person who owns the phone, and does not request inbox or receive-SMS access.
