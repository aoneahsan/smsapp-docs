- **Pakistani mobile numbers only.** Foreign numbers, landlines and short codes are refused.
- **Each SIM has daily and hourly caps.** A SIM starts at 150 messages a day and can rise to 400 as delivery reports come back. Its hourly cap is a fifth of that, and a job waits at least 30 seconds between sends. The Free plan stops at 100 messages a month.
- **No HTTP API.** Other software cannot send through it, so OTP codes and automated sends from your own system are not available.
- **Your SIM and carrier decide the rest.** Whether a message arrives depends on them, and their SMS rate is yours to pay on top of any plan.
