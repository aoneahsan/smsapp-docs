1. **Put your list in a CSV file.** Use whatever already holds the numbers, then follow [Prepare a CSV recipient file](/how-to/auth-and-devices/import-contacts-from-csv).
2. **Check the numbers against what we accept.** Only ordinary Pakistani mobile numbers in the `030`–`036` ranges go through. Everything else is refused with the reason, and there is no override.
3. **Install the app on the phone that will send.** It is a signed APK, not a Google Play app: [Install the Android app](/tutorials/install-the-android-app), and compare the SHA-256 before you open the file.
4. **Sign in and register that phone.** [Sign in with Google](/how-to/auth-and-devices/sign-in-with-google), [register the phone](/how-to/auth-and-devices/register-your-android-device), then [grant its Android permissions](/how-to/auth-and-devices/grant-sms-permissions).
5. **Create a small job first.** [Create a job](/how-to/admin/create-a-job-from-the-admin-dashboard) from the CSV with a few numbers of your own, read the review screen, then release it.
6. **Watch what happens, then send the rest.** [Monitor the job](/how-to/admin/monitor-job-progress). A SIM starts at 150 messages a day and at most 30 an hour, so plan a long list across days ([limits](/reference/quotas-and-limits)).
