# Updates and renewals

Every licence and every buyout includes six months of updates, counted from the day of payment.

## What an update is

An update is a fix or an improvement we make to SMS Mobile App itself: a new screen, a safer queue, a bug fixed on a particular phone. For a licensed copy we build it into the buyer's branded APK and dashboard, and the phones take it over the air. For a buyout it arrives as code to merge.

Changes made only for one buyer are custom work, not updates.

## Renewing

When the included months end, the buyer can renew for 3, 6 or 12 months. A renewal is counted from the later of the renewal date and the day the current updates end, so renewing early loses nothing. Renewals are bought by licence key on the [updates page](https://smsapp.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), with the email the licence was issued to; prices are shown there.

## Without a renewal

The deployment keeps running on the last version it received. Nothing is switched off and no data is removed.
