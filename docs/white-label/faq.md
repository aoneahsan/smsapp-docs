# Questions buyers ask

## Can my customers install it from Google Play?

No. A licensed copy is a signed APK under your name, installed from your own site and updated over the air. We do not publish it to Google Play.

## Is there an iPhone version?

No. iOS does not allow an app to send SMS from the SIM, so there is nothing to build there. The dashboard works on an iPhone.

## Who pays for the text messages?

Whoever owns the SIM. Each phone sends at its carrier's normal SMS rate; we never carry the messages.

## Can it send to numbers outside Pakistan?

Not as it stands. The number policy accepts ordinary Pakistani mobile numbers only. Another country's rules are custom work, tested against real numbers before they ship.

## Do I get the code?

Not with a licence. A [buyout](./what-is-delivered.md#a-buyout-instead) hands over the source of the Android app, the dashboard and the backend.

## Who is responsible for consent and carrier rules?

You and your customers. A licence brings no carrier approval and no right to send in bulk, and we promise no delivery.

## Can you change it for us?

Yes. Changes made only for you are custom work, quoted and paid for separately: [tell us what you need](https://smsapp.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
