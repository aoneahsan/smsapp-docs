# White-label & licensing

A business can run SMS Mobile App under its own name instead of building an SMS sender of its own. We host it, put the buyer's name, logo and colours on the Android app and the web dashboard, connect their domain, and keep it updated. The buyer offers it to their own customers.

There are two ways to have it:

- **A white-label licence.** We run the branded copy. The buyer gets no source code, and we may license the same product to other buyers until someone buys it outright.
- **A buyout.** The buyer receives the source of every repository that builds SMS Mobile App, with six months of updates, and we stop selling it to anyone else.

Prices are on the [white-label price page](https://smsapp.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them from our licensing service. This documentation does not repeat them.

## What stays the same in a licensed copy

A licensed copy is the same product with a different name on it. Everything in the rest of these docs still holds:

- The Android phone is the only sender, through its own SIM. There is no iOS build: iOS does not let an app send SMS from the SIM.
- Only ordinary Pakistani mobile numbers (`030` to `036`) are accepted; everything else is refused with a reason.
- Nothing sends until someone reviews the job and releases it.
- Sent means the phone's modem confirmed the message left. Delivery is never promised.

The buyer and their customers own who they message: recipients' consent, carrier rules and the messaging law where they operate. A licence brings no carrier approval and no right to send in bulk.
