# What a white-label licence delivers

SMS Mobile App sells one platform group: the Android app. It comes with the two parts it cannot work without.

| Part | What the buyer gets |
|---|---|
| Android app | Built and signed under the buyer's name, as their own APK. Their customers install it from the buyer's site and receive updates over the air. It is not published on Google Play. |
| Web dashboard | Hosted on the buyer's domain. Their customers prepare a job, review it and watch what each phone sent. |
| Backend | Accounts, phones, the contact book and the send queue, hosted and maintained by us. |

## Not included

- **An iOS app.** There is none, and an iPhone app could not send SMS from the SIM. The dashboard opens in any browser, an iPhone's included.
- **Source code.** Only a buyout transfers it.
- **Store listings.** We do not publish a licensed copy to Google Play or any other store.
- **The buyer's own costs**: their domain, SIMs, carrier charges and any paid services they choose.

## A buyout instead

A buyout hands over the source code of every repository that builds SMS Mobile App: the Android app, the web dashboard, and the database schema, migrations and server functions behind them. Six months of our updates follow, delivered as code to merge. After a buyout the product is sold to nobody else; licences sold before it keep their terms. The buyer then runs and pays for their own hosting, signing key and accounts. See [the buyout page](https://smsapp.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

Changes made only for one buyer, such as another country's numbering rules, are custom work, quoted separately: [tell us what you need](https://smsapp.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
